Booking & Keyless App Unlock

How does the keyless smart unlock work at campus hubs?

Every CRUIZR vehicle is fitted with an encrypted telematics IoT smart lock. When your booking start time arrives, navigate to the active booking screen on your phone and tap "Unlock Car". The car doors will automatically unlock via Bluetooth/cellular data with no physical key exchange required!

The physical ignition key/start-fob is secured inside the vehicle's glovebox for your trip duration.

What documents are mandatory before car pickup?

You need only two items:

  • An original, valid government Driving License (LMV - Light Motor Vehicle).
  • A valid College Student ID card (or verified university institutional email address).
Can multiple friends take turns driving during our road trip?

Yes! You can add up to 2 co-drivers for free during checkout. Each co-driver must simply upload their valid Driving License prior to departure so that insurance and legal coverage extends to everyone behind the wheel.

Can I extend my trip if we want to stay longer at our destination?

Yes, you can extend your booking right from the app with 1 tap, provided the vehicle does not have an immediate subsequent student reservation. If another booking exists, our support team will help you find a nearby swap option.

Payments, Pricing & Split-Fare

How does the CRUIZR Split-Fare Calculator work?

Our split calculator estimates your total trip cost by combining vehicle daily rental, estimated fuel costs, and FASTag highway tolls. It then computes the exact share per person: Math.ceil(Total Cost ÷ Number of Friends). You can generate a shareable UPI payment link or split QR code to share in your trip WhatsApp group!

Does the prototype demonstrate a zero-security-deposit concept for college students?

Prototype zero-deposit concept: Traditional rental services may block ₹5,000–₹10,000 on cards. This demo illustrates how a valid student ID could be used in a deposit-waiver flow, subject to applicable terms. It is not a real-world guarantee.

What payment methods are supported?

We support all UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, CRED), Net Banking across 50+ Indian banks, and all major Debit/Credit cards. No credit card is required to book.

Cancellations, Weather Delays & Refunds

What is the cancellation timeline and refund policy?

Plans change, especially during college semester schedules! Here is our cancellation structure:

• More than 6 hours prior to trip start: Illustrative full-refund flow, subject to applicable terms.

• 2 to 6 hours prior: Illustrative partial refund or wallet-credit flow, subject to applicable terms.

• Under 2 hours / No show: Incurred preparation fees apply.

How fast do refunds get credited?

UPI payments are refunded in under 2 to 4 hours. Debit and credit cards typically reflect within 2 to 5 business days depending on your issuing bank.

Vehicle Safety, Breakdowns & Damage Protection

What happens if the car has a mechanical breakdown or flat tyre?

The prototype describes 24/7 roadside assistance as a planned support concept. If a real service were available and you experienced a flat tyre, battery drain, or mechanical difficulty:

  1. Pull over to a safe highway shoulder and turn on your hazard lights.
  2. Tap the "SOS Roadside Help" button in your CRUIZR active trip view or call 1800-CRUIZR.
  3. A highway patrol technician is dispatched to your exact GPS coordinates with an estimated arrival of 30–45 minutes.
What is the difference between Standard Cover and the illustrative protection concept?

• Standard Cover (illustrative): Shows a possible ₹5,000 financial-responsibility cap for major external accidental damage, subject to applicable terms.

• Illustrative Protection Concept (+₹199/day): Demonstrates a possible ₹0-deductible option for minor scrapes, small bumper scratches, and windshield rock chips. This is not a real-world guarantee.

Can we take the car across state borders (e.g. Bangalore to Goa or Delhi to Himachal)?

Yes! All CRUIZR fleet cars operate on commercial yellow-on-black tourist vehicle plates with valid All-India Tourist Permits (AITP). You can legally cross state lines. Standard state border road tax receipts can be uploaded at trip end for automatic settlement.

Account & Student Pass Verification

How do I unlock the 25% student discount (STUDENT25)?

Sign in using your mobile number and enter the demo code 123456 (or sign in with your college Google email). Once authenticated, the verified student badge activates in your top navigation bar, and coupon code STUDENT25 is automatically applied at checkout!

Can I change my registered phone number?

Yes, tap your student profile icon in the navigation bar, select "Sign Out", and enter your new mobile number to receive a fresh OTP verification.
