Terms of Use & Agreement

Welcome to CRUIZR ("Platform", "we", "our", or "us"). By browsing our website, booking vehicles, or using our campus mobility services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by these Terms of Use and our Privacy Policy.

CRUIZR operates as a peer-to-peer and managed fleet self-drive platform connecting young adults and college students with verified motor vehicles. All bookings are strictly subject to vehicle availability, identity verification, and compliance with statutory traffic laws of India (Motor Vehicles Act, 1988).

2

User Eligibility & Verification

To reserve and operate any CRUIZR vehicle, you must satisfy all of the following requirements:

  • Minimum Age: You must be at least 18 years of age at the time of reservation.
  • Valid Driving License: You must possess a valid, original Indian Driving License (LMV - Light Motor Vehicle) with no disqualifications. Learner licenses are strictly inadmissible.
  • College Identification: To qualify for student perks, zero security deposit waivers, and the 25% discount, an active College ID Card or verified institution email (.edu / university domain) must be submitted.
  • Digital KYC: You agree to provide a clear selfie and front/back photos of your driving license prior to car unlock.
3

Account & Booking Responsibilities

Each user is responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their login credentials (including OTP codes sent to registered mobile numbers). You agree to immediately notify CRUIZR of any unauthorized access or security breach.

Pre-Trip Inspection Walkaround:

Before unlocking the car, the primary driver must inspect the vehicle and upload 4 photos (all 4 exterior angles + odometer fuel gauge). This protects you from liability for pre-existing scratches or cosmetic scuffs.

Subleasing, renting out, or allowing an unauthorized non-verified individual to drive the vehicle constitutes a material breach of terms and invalidates insurance coverage.

4

Payments, Split-Fare, & Cancellation Policy

All rental fees are displayed in Indian National Rupees (₹) and include applicable Goods and Services Tax (GST).

Cancellation Guidelines

• > 6 Hours before trip start: 100% full refund to original payment source.

• 2 to 6 Hours before trip start: 50% refund or 100% CRUIZR wallet credits.

• < 2 Hours / No-Show: Non-refundable.

Refund Processing Timelines

• UPI / Instant Wallets: Processed within 2 to 4 hours.

• Debit / Credit Cards: 2 to 5 business days per banking protocol.

Zero Security Deposit: Verified college students enjoy zero upfront security deposit. Incidental charges (such as unpaid highway FASTag tolls, state border permits, or late return penalties of ₹250/hour) are settled directly at trip closure.

5

Vehicle Usage, Speed Governors & Safety

Our community relies on responsible driving. The following acts are strictly prohibited and may result in immediate remote vehicle immobilization, blacklisting, and reporting to law enforcement:

  • Driving under the influence of alcohol, narcotics, or prohibited psychotropic substances.
  • Exceeding speed governor thresholds (maximum 80 km/h on expressways as mandated for commercial self-drive plates).
  • Off-roading, river crossings, street racing, or carrying commercial freight.
  • Smoking, vaping, or burning substances inside the vehicle (₹1,500 detailing fine).
6

Privacy Charter & Student Data Protection

Your privacy is of utmost importance. CRUIZR complies with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

What Data We Collect: Contact details (phone number, email), government KYC documents (Driving License, college ID), location telemetry during active trips (GPS coordinates, speed, odometer readings), and payment transaction references.

How We Use Your Data: To authenticate student eligibility, process vehicle dispatch, coordinate emergency 24/7 roadside assistance, and manage insurance claims in case of highway mishaps.

Data Retention & Security: We do not sell your personal information to third-party data brokers. Driving licenses and identity files are encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest with AES-256 standards.

7

Cookies & Client-Side Storage

CRUIZR uses modern browser technologies such as localStorage and temporary session storage to maintain your authentication state, selected campus hub preferences, and friend-split configurations. We do not use intrusive tracking cookies.

Third-Party Services

Our web services utilize trusted external infrastructure partners, including Google Maps Platform (for route calculation and hub coordinates), payment gateways (UPI and Reserve Bank of India compliant aggregators), and telematics tracking partners. These providers are bound by strict data confidentiality covenants.
